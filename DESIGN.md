# M&F Redesign System

Built from `CONTEXT.md`, `DESIGN_PATTERN.md`, and `README.md`.

## Direction

Balanced premium industrial + architectural minimalism. The interface uses large engineered typography, warm neutral surfaces, charcoal structure, restrained M&F yellow, thin borders, asymmetric editorial layouts, and image-led proof areas.

## Tokens

- Brand yellow: `#F2C300`
- Ink: `#171717`
- Charcoal: `#242424`
- Muted text: `#6E6E69`
- Border: `#D8D8D2`
- Warm surface: `#F6F5F0`
- Typography: Archivo for display, Inter for body/UI per the approved design pattern.

## Components Implemented

Navbar, mobile navigation, footer, button variants, container, section, section header, reveal motion, image frame, service card, market card, project card, project listing filters, page hero, CTA section, project inquiry/contact form fields, detail pages, and placeholder-safe content rendering.

## Content Rules

No fabricated business facts are presented as real. Missing facts are marked with `CONTENT REQUIRED — Verify with M&F before launch.` Placeholder photography is labeled in the UI and alt text.

## Accessibility & Behavior

Semantic landmarks, skip link, focus-visible states, persistent form labels, touch-friendly controls, keyboard-friendly navigation, reduced-motion CSS fallback, and non-yellow error/warning indicators are implemented.

## Known Verification Needs

Official M&F brand assets, current contact information, exact service taxonomy, company history, careers, project inventory, authentic imagery, verified metrics, testimonials, certifications, client references, privacy copy, and form submission endpoint.
